DICTATOR — on-device dictation for English and Hinglish
=======================================================

Requirements: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later,
and about 2 GB of free disk space.

1. Drag Dictator into the Applications folder.

2. Open Dictator from Applications.
   macOS will say it "cannot verify" Dictator, because it isn't from the App Store
   or a paid Apple developer account. To open it anyway:
     - Click "Done" on that message.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
       and click "Open Anyway" next to the Dictator message. Confirm with your password.
   You only need to do this once.

3. Follow the Setup screen:
     - Download the speech model (about 600 MB, once).
     - Download the AI cleanup model (about 1.1 GB, recommended).
     - Allow Microphone and Accessibility when asked.
   The first launch spends ~3 minutes optimizing the model for your Mac.

4. Hold the fn key (or the key you picked) in any app, speak, and let go.
   Double-tap the key for hands-free mode. Press Esc to cancel.

Transforms: select text anywhere and press Control+Option+1 (Polish)
or Control+Option+2 (Prompt Engineer).

Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing you say is sent anywhere.
