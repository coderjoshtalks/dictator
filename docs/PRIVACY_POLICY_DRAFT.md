## The short version
Dictator turns your speech into text **on your Mac**. Your voice recordings and the text you dictate are processed
locally and are never sent to us or anyone else.

## What stays on your Mac
- Audio captured while you hold the dictation key (discarded after transcription; optionally the single most recent
  recording is kept locally for troubleshooting, and you can turn this off).
- Transcripts, history, your dictionary and learned corrections, settings.
- To type into other apps and to learn from your corrections, Dictator reads the text field you are typing in through
  macOS Accessibility. This happens only on your Mac.

## What we receive
- **Account and licence**: email address, licence/subscription status, a random device identifier, app version, macOS
  version. Used to provide and verify your subscription.
- **Usage totals** (once a day): number of words and dictations, active minutes, which speech/AI model is in use.
  No audio, no text, no names of the apps you dictate into. [Describe opt-out.]
- **Payments** are handled by [Lemon Squeezy / Paddle], who act as merchant of record; we receive the status of your
  payment, never your card details.
- **Crash reports** (only if you opt in), with personal data removed.

## Why we process it
To provide the service you paid for (contract), to prevent licence abuse (legitimate interest), and to improve the
product with aggregated statistics (consent / legitimate interest, as applicable).

## Retention
Account and billing records: as long as you have an account, plus the period required by tax law.
Usage totals: [e.g. 24 months], then aggregated.

## Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw consent, by emailing [email]. Under India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may also contact our grievance officer at [email]. EU/UK residents have GDPR rights,
including complaining to their supervisory authority.

## Children
Dictator is not directed at children under [13/18].

## Changes
We will notify you in the app before material changes take effect.
